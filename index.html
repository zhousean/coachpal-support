CoachPal

Your basketball coaching notebook: teams and rosters, practice plans, drills, plays you can draw, and your game schedule with scores, notes and game sheet photos.

Support

Questions, problems or ideas? Email coachpal.support@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Common questions

Is my data backed up? Yes. Everything is saved to your account, so it's there when you sign in on another device.

How do I delete my account? Tap the person icon at the top of any tab, then Delete Account. This permanently removes your account and all of its data.
